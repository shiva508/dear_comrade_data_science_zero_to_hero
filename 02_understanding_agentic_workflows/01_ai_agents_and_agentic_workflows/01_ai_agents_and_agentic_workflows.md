# The Modern AI Agent: A System with a "Brain"
### Modern AI agents use a Large Language Models (LLMs) as their central processing unit, or 'brain'. This is what allows agents to understand goals, reason about complex situations, and make decisions.
### But it's more than just a brain. A true agent is a complete system designed to:
- Perceive its environment.
- Reason about what it observes.
- Formulate plans to achieve its goals.
- Execute actions to carry out those plans.
### This ability to perceive, reason, plan, and act with a high degree of autonomy is what separates these agents from simple automation.
## Agentic Workflow or Simple Automation?
### Where do we draw the line between an agentic workflow and standard automation?
### If a system just follows a series of sequential, fixed steps, we're talking about automation, not an agentic workflow. A perfect example is the automated email drip campaign:
1. A user subscribes to a newsletter.
2. The system automatically sends a pre-written "Welcome Email."
3. After 3 days, it automatically sends a pre-written "Follow-up Email."
### This is a classic deterministic workflow. It's efficient for its one specific task, but it cannot adapt or make decisions.
### So, what's the difference in an agent-based system? Instead of having prescribed steps to execute, the agents must decide which steps to take, and how to execute the details of each distinct process they are presented.
